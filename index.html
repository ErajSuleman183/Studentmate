Focus Pomodoro Timer

25:00 STUDY SESSION

Pomodoro Technique Guide

  • 1
    Pick one focused task: Multi-tasking lowers retention. Give one assignment 100% of your attention.
  • 2
    Study for 25 minutes: Turn off social media notifications and stay in deep work until the timer sounds.
  • 3
    Take a 5-minute break: Stand up, stretch, grab water, or look away from screens.
  • 4
    Repeat 4 times: After 4 focus rounds, reward yourself with an extended 15-minute break.
